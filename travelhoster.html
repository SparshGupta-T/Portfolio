Travel Marketplace · B2B / B2C

TravelHoster

A marketplace that turns travel planning into a two-way conversation.

TravelHoster is a web-based travel marketplace connecting travelers directly with verified tour operators. I designed the core experience across mobile and web: custom trip requests ("Tailor Trip"), live quote negotiations with local operators, and the admin management dashboards.

Core Product Pillars

The core of the product: clear pricing and flexible planning for travelers, and a real way for local operators to grow.

1 Browse Tour Packages

Curated multi-day itineraries with clear daily schedules, verified traveler reviews, photos, and upfront pricing. No hidden fees.

2 Customize Your Trip

The "Tailor Trip" request builder lets travelers pick their hotel tier, flight preferences, group size, and flexible dates.

3 Direct Operator Chat

Direct chat between travelers and vetted local operators to ask questions, adjust day-by-day plans, and settle on a price.

4 Smart Request Routing

Routes incoming traveler inquiries to the most qualified operators, based on regional expertise, response time, and past reviews.

Role & What I Built

I focused on keeping the experience simple and consistent across three very different groups: travelers, local tour operators, and site admins.

  • Designed all the core journeys: searching packages, sending custom trip requests, completing a booking.
  • Made sure every feature worked as well on a phone as it did on a desktop, so travelers didn't lose anything on the go.
  • Designed the "Tailor Trip" intake flow and the routing logic that matches traveler requests to the right local operators.
  • Built the tour operator dashboard for tracking leads, sending quotes, confirming bookings, and checking monthly earnings.
  • Built simple admin tools so the operations team could update seasonal banners, featured packages, and pricing themselves, without needing a developer involved.
How Quotes & Negotiations Work

A marketplace only works when both sides are winning: fast responses for travelers, quality leads for operators.

  • Mapped how travelers and operators interact, balancing fast answers for travelers against qualified leads for operators.
  • Set a response window on new inquiries, so travelers get quick answers and leads don't go cold.
  • Designed price breakdowns that show the base trip, taxes, optional activities, and deposit upfront, so there's no guessing.
  • Made the entire package making flow intuitive for Travel Operators.
Project Outcomes

This gave TravelHoster a solid, practical base to launch on. Instead of locking travelers into rigid packages, the platform makes trip planning feel more like talking to a local guide: more flexibility for travelers, more bookings for operators.
