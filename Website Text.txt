Role & Contributions
Product Design Intern · UI/UX, Research & System Design
I contributed to the design of the society management platform by refining and extending an existing base design into a complete, multi-role system. While initial design elements were provided, I restructured layouts and flows to ensure consistency, usability, and scalability across the platform.

I worked across all key user roles, including residents, gatekeepers, and administrators, with full ownership of the admin and superadmin dashboards. I designed core flows such as QR-based visitor entry, reusable and express passes for frequent visitors (e.g., delivery personnel), and approval workflows where residents can pre-authorize guests or respond to gatekeeper requests in real time.

I also contributed to defining the system logic behind visitor handling, including scanned entry via the gatekeeper app, request-based approvals for unknown visitors, and structured tracking of all entries and exits. In addition, I designed features like maintenance payments, notification systems, polls, and notice boards to improve communication and engagement within the society, along with an SOS feature for emergency situations.

I was also involved in early explorations for a V2 of the platform, focusing on extending functionality and improving system scalability, although it was not taken forward.

Overall, my work focused on aligning user experience with system logic, delivering a cohesive platform that supports secure access control, efficient operations, and community engagement.
