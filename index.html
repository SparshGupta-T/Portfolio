Deep Learning · Computer Vision & Edge AI Lead Researcher & Systems Developer

RecycleNet

Deep Learning-Assisted Dynamic Plastic Identification Technique For Automated Waste Segregation

An edge-optimized computer vision system trained on 2,420 custom images and 8,884 annotations to classify 6 industrial resin codes (PET, HDPE, PVC, LDPE, PP, PS) in real time. Delivers 2.0 ms inference latency on edge devices and 62.0% mAP@50 with RF-DETR transformers. Deployed via Python Flask/OpenCV desktop streaming and an offline native Kotlin/Jetpack Compose Android app with PyTorch Lite. Published in IEEE ICCECE 2026.

I'm a product-focused engineer working across hardware, software, and systems end to end. Over the last 3.5 years, I've built products from scratch across different domains — not just designing them, but working out how they actually function underneath: user flows, backend logic, data.

Figma, Framer, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and C make up most of my toolkit, alongside hands-on experience with control systems — Metasys, SCT, CCT, Solution Navigator — from my time at Johnson Controls. There's also a published IEEE paper on a vision-based model for plastic waste detection.
