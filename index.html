I’m a product-focused engineer working across hardware, software, and systems end-to-end. Over the past 3.5 years, I’ve worked on products from scratch across different domains, focusing not just on design but on how things actually function—user flows, backend logic, and data.

I work with Figma, Framer, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and C, and also have hands-on experience with control systems (Metasys, SCT, CCT, Solution Navigator) from my time at Johnson Controls. I’ve also published an IEEE paper on a vision-based model for plastic waste detection.
