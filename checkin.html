CheckIn

Fast and secure check-ins for residential communities

CheckIn is a community and visitor management app for residential societies. I took early wireframes and turned them into a full system: QR entry passes for guests, instant approval alerts on resident phones, outdoor tablet screens for security guards, monthly maintenance billing, and emergency SOS alerts.

Core Product Pillars

Balancing tight gate security with everyday convenience for residents and delivery workers — these are the pieces that make that work.

1 Fast QR Entry Passes

Residents generate a QR pass and send it over WhatsApp, so guests and delivery riders can scan in at the gate in seconds.

2 Resident Approval Alerts

When an unexpected visitor shows up, the guard logs their details and the resident gets an instant notification to approve or decline.

3 Society Dues & Maintenance

Monthly maintenance billing, digital receipts, payment reminders, and records the society committee can actually rely on.

4 Guard Tablet & Admin Portal

A big-button tablet app for guards working outdoors in bright sunlight, paired with a web dashboard for admins.

Role & What I Built

I owned the product across four user groups: residents, security guards, facility managers, and committee admins.

  • Worked across all four groups to find where people got stuck day to day, at the gate and inside the society.
  • Took full design ownership of the Admin and Superadmin portals, building out visitor logs and clubhouse booking tools.
  • Designed fast entry passes for guests, daily domestic help (cooks, cleaners, drivers), and quick delivery drop-offs.
  • Designed an SOS button that alerts the security gate and nearby neighbours with one tap.
  • Built community tools for notices, maintenance complaints, clubhouse bookings, and resident polls.
Gate Check-in Speed & Guard Usability

During morning and evening rush hours, any delay at the gate backs up traffic fast. Every extra tap matters.

  • Replaced slow paper logbooks with QR scanning, cutting gate wait times from minutes down to seconds.
  • Made sure the guard tablet keeps working even if the gate Wi-Fi drops, syncing entries once the connection is back.
  • Kept resident phone numbers and personal details hidden from delivery staff, while still keeping the security log intact.
  • Tested the interface with guards standing in direct sunlight: big buttons, high contrast, clear confirmation sounds.
Project Outcomes

Paper registers at the gate are gone. Guards move visitors through without hassle, residents know exactly who's coming up, and society managers have a proper log of everything instead of a logbook nobody can search.
