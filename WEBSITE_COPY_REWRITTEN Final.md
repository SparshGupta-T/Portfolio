# 13. Case Study: SupportFit

### [SUPPORTFIT.HERO.OVERVIEW]
SupportFit, built with Supportroom, is a workplace wellness app that helps employees build real habits around movement, sleep, and nutrition. I led the end-to-end design: the user flows, the nutrition logic (BMR, BMI, calorie burn), daily activity tracking, and therapist consultation bookings.

### [SUPPORTFIT.PILLARS.SECTION_DESC]
What the app is really built around — the pieces that help employees build daily routines that stick.

### [SUPPORTFIT.PILLARS.PILLAR_1_DESC]
Syncs automatically with Apple Health, Google Fit, and wearables. Steps, heart rate, and sleep come in without any manual entry.

### [SUPPORTFIT.PILLARS.PILLAR_2_DESC]
Turns daily activity numbers into simple weekly summaries, so people can see their sleep, active days, and recovery without feeling buried in data.

### [SUPPORTFIT.PILLARS.PILLAR_3_DESC]
Chat, voice notes, and video calls with qualified coaches and nutritionists, with easy booking and follow-ups built in.

### [SUPPORTFIT.PILLARS.PILLAR_4_DESC]
Daily calorie and water goals based on a person's actual height, weight, and activity level, not generic averages.

### [SUPPORTFIT.NARRATIVE.BLOCK_1_TITLE]
Role & What I Built

### [SUPPORTFIT.NARRATIVE.BLOCK_1_DESC]
I worked closely with the clinical team and the developers to make sure every screen had a proper flow, covered its edge cases, and got the calculation logic right.

### [SUPPORTFIT.NARRATIVE.BLOCK_1_BULLET_1]
Worked with certified therapists to turn their wellness advice into features people would actually want to use every day.

### [SUPPORTFIT.NARRATIVE.BLOCK_1_BULLET_2]
Redesigned the full flow, from the first-time setup quiz all the way to booking a 1-on-1 session.

### [SUPPORTFIT.NARRATIVE.BLOCK_1_BULLET_3]
Led the V2 redesign by finding where people were dropping off and adding light motivation: streaks, badges, company-wide step challenges.

### [SUPPORTFIT.NARRATIVE.BLOCK_1_BULLET_4]
Designed in-app messaging, voice notes, and post-session follow-up notes between coaches and users.

### [SUPPORTFIT.NARRATIVE.BLOCK_1_BULLET_5]
Built the Resource Center UI and organized workout videos, breathing exercises, and articles into categories people could actually find things in.

### [SUPPORTFIT.NARRATIVE.BLOCK_2_TITLE]
Calorie & Nutrition Logic

### [SUPPORTFIT.NARRATIVE.BLOCK_2_DESC]
Health numbers have to be trustworthy. Every recommendation in the app runs on standard nutrition formulas, not guesswork.

### [SUPPORTFIT.NARRATIVE.BLOCK_2_BULLET_1]
Mapped calorie burn based on height, weight, age, and how active someone really is day to day, not how active they say they are in a form.

### [SUPPORTFIT.NARRATIVE.BLOCK_2_BULLET_2]
Used the Mifflin-St Jeor BMR formula and standard BMI calculations, so users get targets built for them instead of rough averages.

### [SUPPORTFIT.NARRATIVE.BLOCK_2_BULLET_3]
Adjusted daily calorie targets automatically whenever someone logged a workout or extra steps.

### [SUPPORTFIT.NARRATIVE.BLOCK_2_BULLET_4]
Ran user tests until logging a meal or water took just 2 to 3 taps. Long forms are usually why people stop tracking altogether.

### [SUPPORTFIT.NARRATIVE.BLOCK_3_TITLE]
Project Outcomes

### [SUPPORTFIT.NARRATIVE.BLOCK_3_TEXT]
These changes turned SupportFit into something people could actually use daily. Pairing solid nutrition logic with simple, friendly screens gave employees a tool they could open, check, and log in seconds.
